# Snow leopard

The snow leopard (*Panthera uncia*, also historically classified as
*Uncia uncia*) inhabits the mountain ranges of Central Asia.[^taxonomy][^biology] It favors
steep, rocky terrain and alpine or subalpine habitats. Large paws help it
travel over snow, while its long tail assists balance and can cover its
extremities in cold weather.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Snow Leopard is the name associated with Mac OS X 10.6, following
[Leopard](Leopard.md) and preceding [Lion](Lion.md).[^macos]

# Leopard

The leopard (*Panthera pardus*) is a cat native to parts of Africa and
Asia. Its coat usually has dark rosettes, and its strong shoulders and limbs
support climbing. Leopards occupy varied habitats, including forests,
savannas, mountains, and scrubland.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Leopard is the name associated with Mac OS X 10.5.[^macos]

## Related animals

A melanistic leopard is commonly called a black [panther](Panther.md).
The [snow leopard](Snow%20leopard.md) has a separate entry in this collection.[^biology]

# Tiger

The tiger (*Panthera tigris*) is a large cat native to Asia. Its historical
range extended across much of the continent, but it has disappeared from
many former habitats. Tigers inhabit varied environments, including tropical
forests, mangrove swamps, grasslands, and cold forests.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Tiger is the name associated with Mac OS X 10.4, between
[Panther](Panther.md) and [Leopard](Leopard.md) in Apple's release sequence.[^macos]

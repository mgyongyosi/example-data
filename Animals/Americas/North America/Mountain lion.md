# Mountain lion

The mountain lion (*Puma concolor*) is the same species as the
[puma](Puma.md), also known as the cougar. It is a large, slender
American cat with a long tail and a generally plain brownish coat. Despite
its common name, it inhabits forests, grasslands, swamps, and scrubland as
well as mountains.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Mountain Lion is the name associated with OS X 10.8. Apple had previously
used Puma for Mac OS X 10.1, so these two releases refer to the same
animal species under different common names.[^macos][^biology]

## Related animals

The [lion](Lion.md) has its own entry and a different scientific name,
*Panthera leo*.

# Jaguar

The jaguar (*Panthera onca*) is the largest cat native to the Americas.
It has a powerful build, broad jaws, and a coat usually marked with dark
rosettes. Jaguars favor habitats with water, dense cover, and sufficient
prey, and they are capable swimmers.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Jaguar is the name associated with Mac OS X 10.2.[^macos]

## Related animals

Melanistic jaguars have a dark coat in which the spots may still be visible.
They are among the cats commonly called black [panthers](Panther.md).[^biology]

# Cheetah

The cheetah (*Acinonyx jubatus*) is a slender cat with long legs, a small
head, round black spots, and dark stripes running from its eyes toward its
mouth. It favors open habitats such as grasslands and deserts. Its historical
range included much of Africa and parts of Asia.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Cheetah is the name associated with Mac OS X 10.0, the first release in the
cat-named sequence covered by this collection.[^macos]

## Related animals

Unlike the rosette markings of the [leopard](Leopard.md), a cheetah's spots
are solid.[^biology]

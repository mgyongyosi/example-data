# Puma

The puma (*Puma concolor*), also called a cougar or mountain lion, is a
large American cat. Adults generally have a plain yellowish-brown to
grayish-brown coat, muscular limbs, and a long tail. The species occupies
habitats ranging from forests and grasslands to mountains and scrubland.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Puma is the name associated with Mac OS X 10.1.[^macos]

## Related animals

[Mountain lion](Mountain%20lion.md) is another common name for the same species,
not a separate species. Apple later used that name for OS X 10.8.[^biology][^macos]

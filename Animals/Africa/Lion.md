# Lion

The lion (*Panthera leo*) is a large cat found in parts of sub-Saharan
Africa and in India's Gir region. African lions inhabit plains and
savannas where prey and cover are available. Their historical range also
included northern Africa and much of southwestern Asia.[^biology]

## Mac OS namesake

Lion is the name associated with OS X 10.7.[^macos]

## Related animals

Despite its name, the [mountain lion](Mountain%20lion.md) is the
same species as the [puma](Puma.md), not *Panthera leo*.

# Mars

## Summary
Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. It is a cold, rocky world known for its reddish surface, caused by iron oxide (rust) in its soil.[^nasa-mars]

## Facts
- **Solar distance:** about 228 million km (1.52 AU) on average.[^nasa-mars]
- **Diameter:** about 6,779 km.[^nasa-mars]
- **Day:** about 24 hours, 37 minutes.[^nasa-mars]
- **Year:** about 687 Earth days.[^nasa-mars]
- **Moons:** Phobos and Deimos.[^nasa-mars]
- **Atmosphere:** thin and composed mostly of carbon dioxide.[^nasa-mars]

## Surface
Mars has impact craters, ancient river channels, and large volcanoes. Olympus Mons is the largest known volcano in the Solar System, and Valles Marineris is a vast canyon system. Evidence indicates that liquid water flowed on the surface in the distant past.[^nasa-mars]

## Exploration
Orbiters, landers, and rovers have studied Mars. Current exploration investigates its geology and climate and searches for signs that microbial life may have existed there.[^nasa-mars]

# Saturnus

## Overview

Saturnus, known in English as Saturn, is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in the Solar System. It is a gas giant composed primarily of hydrogen and helium.[^nasa-saturn][^britannica-saturn]

## Physical characteristics

- **Equatorial diameter:** approximately 120,536 km.[^nasa-saturn]
- **Mean distance from the Sun:** approximately 1.43 billion km (9.58 AU).[^nasa-saturn]
- **Orbital period:** approximately 29.4 Earth years.[^nasa-saturn]
- **Rotation period:** approximately 10.7 hours; estimates vary by measurement.[^nasa-saturn]
- **Natural satellites:** numerous confirmed moons; the number changes as discoveries are confirmed.[^nasa-saturn]

## Rings and atmosphere

Saturn has a prominent ring system made mostly of water-ice particles, along with dust and rocky material. The rings are broad but relatively thin. Its atmosphere is mostly hydrogen and helium and features bands of clouds, strong winds, and a persistent hexagonal jet-stream pattern around the north pole.[^nasa-saturn]

## Exploration

The Cassini spacecraft studied Saturn and its system from 2004 to 2017. Its Huygens probe landed on Saturn's moon Titan in 2005.[^nasa-saturn]

# Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is a rocky planet similar in size to Earth, with a dense atmosphere composed mainly of carbon dioxide and clouds of sulfuric acid. A powerful greenhouse effect makes its surface the hottest of any planet in the Solar System.[^nasa-venus]

## Facts

- **Order from the Sun:** 2.[^nasa-venus]
- **Average distance from the Sun:** about 108 million km (0.72 AU).[^nasa-venus]
- **Diameter:** about 12,104 km.[^nasa-venus]
- **Orbital period:** about 225 Earth days.[^nasa-venus]
- **Rotation period:** about 243 Earth days; Venus rotates retrograde.[^nasa-venus]
- **Moons:** 0.[^nasa-venus]
- **Average surface temperature:** about 465 °C.[^nasa-venus]

Venus has a rocky surface with volcanic plains, mountains, and impact craters. Its surface atmospheric pressure is about 92 times Earth's sea-level pressure. Seen from Earth, Venus is often called the morning star or evening star because it appears brightest near sunrise or sunset.[^nasa-venus]
